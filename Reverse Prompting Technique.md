**Your Task:**
Reverse-engineer the exact prompt that produced this output. The generated prompt MUST strictly follow the **RTCFR Framework**:
- **R (Role):** Persona and expertise required.
- **T (Task):** Objective and detailed steps expected.
- **C (Context):** Deduced user attributes (age, veg/non-veg, medical conditions, goals).
- **F (Few-Shots):** 1 or 2 representative input/output exemplars that match this style.
- **R (Response Format):** Layout, tone, sub-headings, and constraints used in the target output.
